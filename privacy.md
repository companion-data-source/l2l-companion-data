# Privacy Policy — Convention Companion ("The Unofficial L2L App")

*Effective August 15, 2026*

Convention Companion is built for families. The shortest honest summary: **your family's information stays with your family.** We run no servers, require no accounts, and collect no data.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter — your kids, their events, tracked work, photos, awards, registration details — is stored **on your own device** and, if you are signed into iCloud, synced through **your own private iCloud database**. That iCloud data belongs to your Apple Account; we cannot see it, and Apple's own privacy protections govern it. Photos you attach are kept at full resolution on your devices and in your iCloud — they are never uploaded to us, because there is no "us" to upload to.

## What the app sends over the internet

One thing: at launch, the app checks a **public file** (hosted in this repository) for updated convention dates, deadlines, and official links, so a changed date can reach you without an app update. This request sends nothing about you or your family — it is an ordinary file download, no different from loading a public web page. If it fails, the app quietly uses its built-in dates.

There are no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs, and no crash reporters in the app.

## What you choose to share

The app can build files **you** send at your own choosing:

- **Registration packets and reports** you share with your congregation's L2L coordinator (PDF and app-readable files) — these go wherever you send them, by whatever means you choose (AirDrop, Messages, email).
- **Family snapshots** you share between your own family's devices.

The app never sends anything on its own. Every share ends in a system share sheet or a pre-addressed message that you review and send yourself.

## Children

The app is designed to be used by parents and group leaders. All information about children stays in the family's own storage as described above. We collect nothing from anyone — adult or child.

## App Store privacy label

**Data Not Collected.** No data is collected, sold, shared, or used for tracking, by us or anyone else.

## Changes

If a future version of the app changes any of this, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be visible in this repository's public edit history.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue in this repository.
